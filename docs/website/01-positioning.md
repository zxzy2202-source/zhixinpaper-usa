# Positioning

## Best-fit customer

Thermal paper distributors, importers, POS consumable suppliers, managed service providers, and private-label buyers in Europe, the United States, Canada, and Mexico who need repeatable specifications, evidence-aware compliance options, and reliable multi-SKU replenishment.

## Competitive frame

The practical alternatives are generic catalog suppliers, price-only marketplaces, and broad paper sites that make buyers infer compatibility and evidence from a product name.

## Differentiated value

Zhixinpaper.com organizes thermal paper sourcing around a specification-led qualification path:

1. Select the buying task: core volume, compliance, performance, custom, or system-qualified.
2. Define the SKU across L0-L7.
3. Connect the quoted grade, documents, sample, and test conditions to the order.

This is a process promise, not an unsupported claim that every grade or device is already approved.

## Positioning statement

For repeat-order thermal paper buyers who cannot afford specification, compatibility, or evidence gaps, Zhixin Paper provides a qualification-led route from device and roll dimensions to grade, documents, printing, packaging, and sample approval.

## Positioning quality

- Relevance: 9/10. It addresses the highest-priority buyer risks found in the knowledge base.
- Distinctness: 8/10. The specification-led path is clearer than a flat catalog, but proof assets must continue to improve.
- Credibility: 8/10. The wording stays within documented process and evidence boundaries.
- Page fit: 9/10. The message maps directly to the product hub, quote fields, guides, and compliance owner.
