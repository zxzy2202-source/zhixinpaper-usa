- `zhixinpaper.com` serves thermal paper and thermal label buyers in Europe, the United States, Canada, and Mexico.
- Product H1s remain product-, specification-, or task-led. Regional context belongs in terminology, compliance, FAQ, delivery, metadata, and market routes.
- `zxpapers.com` owns the broader portfolio and other global markets, including NCR forms and additional label categories.

## Evidence boundary

- Product hierarchy describes how to qualify an offer; it does not prove that every option is currently manufacturable.
- Compliance, durability, image-life, compatibility, MOQ, lead-time, and capacity claims require grade- or project-specific evidence.
- `waterproof`, `three-proof`, and fixed-year archival claims are not standard promises. Name the exposure and test conditions.
- Jumbo reels remain capability-gated until width, GSM, roll weight, joins, packing, loading, and supply stability are confirmed.

## Current implementation decision

- Preserve every existing product canonical.
- Use T1-T5 as navigation and decision paths, not as five new URL folders.
- Keep T2 and T3 as composable compliance and performance attributes.
- Group the existing 11 roll pages into core volume, custom/value-added, and system-qualified routes.
